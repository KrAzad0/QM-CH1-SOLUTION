01 — CHAPTER ROADMAPGRIFFITHS / SCHROETER · THIRD EDITION

Six core topics.
One framework.

The time-independent Schrödinger equation is a boundary-value problem. Its energy spectrum and spatial eigenfunctions depend on the potential, self-adjoint boundary conditions, and normalization.

This companion follows the third-edition problem numbering, adds intermediate mathematics, and distinguishes bound states, scattering states, and numerical approximations.

2.1

Stationary states

Separation of variables, discrete spectra and superpositions.

\(\Psi_n=\psi_ne^{-iE_nt/\hbar}\)
2.2

Infinite square well

Quantization, orthogonality and time evolution.

\(E_n \propto n^2\)
2.3

Harmonic oscillator

Ladder operators, Hermite polynomials and coherent dynamics.

\(E_n=(n+\tfrac12)\hbar\omega\)
2.4

Free particles

Fourier transforms and dispersing wave packets.

\(\omega(k)=\hbar k^2/2m\)
2.5

Delta potentials

Singular interactions, bound levels and jumps in derivatives.

\(\Delta\psi' \propto \psi(0)\)
2.6

Finite square well

Even/odd bound states, scattering and tunneling.

\(T+R=1\)

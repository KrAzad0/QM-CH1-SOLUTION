01 — THE STRUCTURE OF QUANTUM THEORYSIX CONCEPT CLUSTERS

From wavefunctions
to abstract states.

Chapter 3 reorganizes the first two chapters into a general mathematical language. Square-integrable states live in Hilbert space, Hermitian operators describe observables, and their eigenvectors organize measurement probabilities.

Its later exercises go beyond the basic formalism: coherent states, quantum time scales, three-level systems, supersymmetry and the essential role of operator domains.

3.1

Hilbert space

Square integrability, inner products, normalization and complete bases.

\(\langle f|g\rangle=\int f^*g\,dx\)
3.2

Observables

Adjoints, Hermitian operators, eigenvalues and their spectra.

\(\hat Q^\dagger=\hat Q\)
3.3

Eigenfunctions

Discrete and continuous spectra, orthogonality and completeness.

\(\hat Q|q\rangle=q|q\rangle\)
3.4

Statistical interpretation

Momentum distributions and measurement amplitudes in different bases.

\(P(q)=|\langle q|\psi\rangle|^2\)
3.5

Uncertainty

Commutators, standard deviations and energy–time relations.

\(\sigma_A\sigma_B\geq\tfrac12|\langle[A,B]\rangle|\)
3.6

Vectors and operators

Dirac notation, matrices, unitary dynamics and coherent states.

\(|\psi\rangle=\sum_n c_n|n\rangle\)

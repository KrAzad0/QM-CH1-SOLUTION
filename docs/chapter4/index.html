01 — THE STRUCTURE OF QUANTUM THEORYSIX CONCEPT CLUSTERS

From spatial waves
to quantum spin.

Chapter 4 extends the Schrödinger equation to three dimensions. Separation in spherical coordinates reveals angular momentum quantization and the exact Coulomb spectrum.

Its later exercises develop spin one-half, coupled angular momenta, Stern–Gerlach dynamics, minimal coupling, numerical confined hydrogen and the Aharonov–Bohm effect.

4.1

Three-dimensional Schrödinger equation

Cartesian separation, central potentials, radial boundary conditions.

\(\hat H=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2+V\)
4.2

Hydrogen atom

Radial Coulomb solutions, orbital shapes, discrete spectral energies.

\(E_n=-13.6\,\mathrm{eV}/n^2\)
4.3

Orbital angular momentum

Commutator algebra, spherical harmonics and ladder operators.

\(L^2=\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)\)
4.4

Spin and addition

Pauli matrices, spinors, singlets, triplets and Clebsch–Gordan coupling.

\(S_i=\hbar\sigma_i/2\)
4.5

Electromagnetic interactions

Gauge transformations, Landau levels and magnetic-flux interference.

\(\mathbf p\mapsto\mathbf p-q\mathbf A\)
4.6

Advanced solutions

Numerical spherical wells, hydrogen confinement, spin correlations and scattering.

\(\Delta\varphi=q\Phi/\hbar\)

01 — THE STRUCTURE OF MANY-PARTICLE THEORYSIX PROBLEM GROUPS

From identical states
to quantum matter.

Chapter 5 studies the fundamentally different ways bosons and fermions combine. Exchange symmetry determines the allowed states, spin configurations, and microscopic organization of matter.

Its later problems apply that structure to helium, the periodic table, Fermi statistics, band gaps, electron magnetism, white dwarfs, and phonons in crystals.

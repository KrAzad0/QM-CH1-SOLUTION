01 — INTRODUCTIONTHE PHYSICS BEHIND THE SYMBOLS

One chapter.
Six foundations.

The opening chapter establishes how a quantum state evolves and how measurements are extracted from it. It moves from the Schrödinger equation to Born's interpretation, probability, normalization, momentum and uncertainty.

These are independent study notes: original derivations, intermediate algebra, limiting-case checks and comments on the physical meaning of each result.

1.1

Schrödinger equation

How a wavefunction evolves with time.

\(i\hbar\partial_t\Psi=\hat H\Psi\)
1.2

Statistical interpretation

What the square of an amplitude means.

\(\rho=|\Psi|^2\)
1.3

Probability

Distributions, averages, and spread.

\(\langle x\rangle=\int x\rho\,dx\)
1.4

Normalization

The particle must be found somewhere.

\(\int |\Psi|^2 dx=1\)
1.5

Momentum

Observables as mathematical operators.

\(\hat p=-i\hbar\partial_x\)
1.6

Uncertainty principle

A fundamental constraint on state widths.

\(\sigma_x\sigma_p\geq\hbar/2\)
